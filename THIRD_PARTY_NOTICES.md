## Indic Tiny TTS: Hindi

Kavya AI's optional offline Hindi voice uses the Indic Tiny TTS: Hindi model by Sulabh Katiyar.

- Model: https://huggingface.co/sulabhkatiyar/indic-tiny-tts-hindi
- Acoustic model weights and ONNX text encoder/flow decoder: CC BY 4.0.
- Training dataset: Hindi portion of Rasa by AI4Bharat, also CC BY 4.0: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/Rasa
- Dataset citation: Praveen Srinivasa Varadhan, Ashwin Sankar, Giri Raju, and Mitesh M. Khapra. “Rasa: Building Expressive Speech Synthesis Systems for Indian Languages in Low-resource Settings.” INTERSPEECH 2024.

The ONNX vocoder is an unchanged ONNX conversion of charactr/vocos-mel-24khz, revision 0feb3fdd929bcd6649e0e7c5a688cf7dd012ef21, and is licensed under the MIT License by its authors.

- Upstream: https://github.com/gemelo-ai/vocos
- Model source: https://huggingface.co/charactr/vocos-mel-24khz
- Citation: Hubert Siuzdak. “Vocos: Closing the gap between time-domain and Fourier-based neural vocoders for high-quality audio synthesis.” ICLR 2024.
